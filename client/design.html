Theme

Three states, and the absence of a data-theme attribute means “system”. Open this page in two windows and change the theme in one: the other follows, through the storage event. The writing window does not receive it — that is why choisir() also applies locally.

Veils — six, out of theme

They are laid over the video, whose content follows no theme: they are therefore identical in light and in dark, and they are outside the fifty-two contrast pairs — their readability depends on an image that cannot be known.
